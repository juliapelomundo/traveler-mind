## What is Traveler Mind?

Traveler Mind is an AI-powered travel planning platform that creates personalized itineraries based on a traveler's budget, available time, and interests.

## Why I Built It

After traveling extensively, I noticed how difficult it can be to plan a trip using information scattered across different platforms.

Generic itineraries often fail to account for what actually matters to each traveler: how much they want to spend, how much time they have, and what they want to experience.

I started building Traveler Mind to explore a more personalized approach to travel planning.

## What I Built

I built a functional product prototype with Base44 that uses AI to transform a traveler's preferences and constraints into a personalized itinerary.

The platform focuses on:

* Budget
* Trip duration
* Interests
* Personalized recommendations
* Itinerary planning
